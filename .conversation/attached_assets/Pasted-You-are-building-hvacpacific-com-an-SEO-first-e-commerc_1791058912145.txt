## 4. Product import script (`/scripts/import-products.ts`)

Input: `hvacpacific_product_list.xlsx` (I will upload it to the repo). Read tabs **`Units`** and **`Accessories`** by exact header name.

- **Units columns:** listing_id, status, site_category, brand, title, tonnage, system_type, outdoor_model, indoor_model, furnace_model, refrigerant, efficiency_stated, ahri_number, seer2, eer2, hspf2, price, cost, margin, ship_weight_lb, flags, action, compliant_alternative
- **Accessories columns:** sku, status, site_category, subcategory, brand, title, google_title, key_specs, price, cost, margin, qty, ship_weight_lb, shipping_scope, flags, action, source_category, source_brand, search_keywords

Rules:
- Upsert by SKU (`listing_id` for units, `sku` for accessories). Idempotent — re-running updates, never duplicates.
- `status` PUBLISH / PUBLISH AFTER CHECK / KEEP - LEGAL RISK → Shopify ACTIVE and published to Headless channel. NEEDS DATA / HOLD → DRAFT, not published.
- Vendor = `brand` (house brand = `hvacpacific`). Skip publishing any row whose brand is `DECIDE` (force DRAFT) and log it.
- Price from `price`; cost → Shopify "cost per item". Empty price → DRAFT regardless of status, logged.
- `efficiency_stated` is **internal only** — do not map it to seer2 or display it.
- `flags` and `action` → private product note metafield (`internal.flags`), never rendered.
- Refrigerant rows → `requires_epa608 = true`, tag `pickup-only`.
- Units (site_category in AC + Furnace Systems, Heat Pump Systems, Packaged Units, Ductless Mini Splits) → `requires_licensed_install = true`.
- Rows where system_type contains "3-phase" → `three_phase = true`.
- Accessory rows whose flags mention Prop 65 → `prop65 = true`.
- Create/attach collections from site_category and subcategory (see section 5).
- Output a CSV report: created / updated / drafted / skipped + reason.

## 8. Google Merchant Center feed

Route `/feeds/google-merchant.xml` (RSS 2.0 with `g:` namespace), regenerated on revalidation. One feed for English (Chinese feed later).

Fields: `g:id` (SKU/listing_id), `g:title` (= `google_title` metafield, else product title, ≤150 chars), `g:description` (plain text), `g:link` (hvacpacific.com product URL — never the Shopify domain), `g:image_link`, `g:availability`, `g:price` ("4550.00 USD"), `g:condition` new, `g:brand`, `g:mpn` (model # for brand-name products), `g:identifier_exists` = `no` for house-brand items without GTIN/MPN, `g:is_bundle` = `yes` for multi-component systems (brand/mpn = outdoor unit), `g:google_product_category`, `g:product_type` (site breadcrumb path), `g:product_highlight` (up to 10, from highlights), `g:shipping` reflecting local delivery/pickup.

Exclude from feed: DRAFT products, products with no image, anything with `requires_epa608 = true` (refrigerant), and HOLD/NEEDS DATA.

Also expose `/feeds/local-inventory.xml` scaffold (store_code, id, availability, price) behind a config flag for later free local listings once the showroom is set.
